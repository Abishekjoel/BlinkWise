Severity grading

Five stages of diabetic retinopathy

BlinkWise classifies every image into one of the standard grades, from healthy to proliferative.

Grade 0

No DR

No visible signs of retinopathy.

Grade 1

Mild

Tiny microaneurysms begin to appear.

Grade 2

Moderate

Blocked vessels, haemorrhages and exudates.

Grade 3

Severe

Widespread blockage, high risk of progression.

Grade 4

Proliferative

New fragile vessels threaten vision.
